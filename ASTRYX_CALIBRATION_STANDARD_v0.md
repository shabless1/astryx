## 1. What this is

The Astryx Calibration Standard is a reference method for turning a person's **birth data** and the **live sky** into a **deterministic, embodied calibration** — a specific sequence of sound, breath, scent, taste, color, and field work that can be performed with physical planetary tuning forks.

It is a **calibration** method, not a prediction method. It reads a natal chart as a structured pattern map, never as a fortune. It produces the **same output for the same input, every time**, and every output is versioned so it can be reproduced and audited.

The Standard exists so that the method can be **named, cited, and depended on** — by people, by practitioners, and by the software agents that increasingly act on their behalf.

**What it is not.** The Standard is an observational and wellness-calibration reference. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prescribe, and it is not a substitute for care from a licensed professional. All of its language is probabilistic — *may suggest, may correlate with, is traditionally associated with* — by design.

## 4. The 6-Sense Protocol

Every calibration produces one protocol keyed to the carrier planet, expressed through six channels:

1. **Sound** — the fork and frequency, and where on the body it is applied.
2. **Scent** — botanical aromatics associated with the carrier.
3. **Taste** — a botanical infusion associated with the carrier.
4. **Body** — a breath pattern, a posture or placement, and a movement.
5. **Sight** — a color, and where indicated a geometric form.
6. **Field** — the auric / etheric field around the body, which the fork's vibration moves through and clears. Off-body ("field") forks work here directly; on-body ("weighted") forks reach it through the bone.

Channels 1–5 are selected by the signal's polarity state so the whole protocol moves toward balance. Channel 6 is universal: it is the effect of sound spreading through the surrounding field, present in every calibration.

Every protocol carries a safety layer: any botanical, mineral, or crystal referenced must surface its safety note, and malachite is always flagged (polished and sealed only; never raw; never ingested).

## 5. The rules (invariants)

A system conforming to the Standard enforces all of the following.

**5.1 Never-amplify.** A signal in *excess* or *blocked* state is never sounded as itself. It is met with its regulator's frequency. Signals that are *deficient* or *balanced* are activated directly.

**5.2 Balance guarantee.** Every session includes at least one activating or resourcing frequency. No calibration is pure suppression.

**5.3 Earth bookends.** Every session opens on the Earth Day tone (grounding) and closes on an Earth tone (grounding). The chakra sequence seals on Earth Day.

**5.4 Activation is never the close.** Mars, an activating tone, is never placed at the end of a session.

**5.5 Depth is always grounded.** Pluto, the deepest tone, is always followed by Earth grounding.

**5.6 Womb-safe placement.** Intimate and pelvic regions are guided as an **off-body sweep** — no contact — never as an on-body point.

**5.7 Two strikes, then let it fade.** A fork is applied at most twice per phase, on a soft surface (never metal on metal), and allowed to ring out fully.

**5.8 Determinism.** Same input + same Standard version = same output, byte for byte. The method uses no randomness and no hidden clock; the calibration date is an explicit input.

**5.9 Versioning.** Every output is stamped with the Standard version that produced it. Rules and mappings change only by version.

**5.10 Astryx does not calibrate.** The guide explains; it never computes, selects, or configures a session. Any session it offers is one the method already produced.

**5.11 Compliance.** Every output — to a person or to a machine — carries the reference-not-medical framing and probabilistic language. (§9)

## 8. The machine interface

The Standard is designed to be depended on by software.

**8.1 Capabilities.** A conforming provider exposes, at minimum:
- `calibrate` — birth data (+ optional intake and the calibration date) → carrier planet, signal hierarchy with polarity states, temperature, the 6-Sense Protocol, and the session sequence;
- `fork-map` — a planet (+ optional chart) → its frequency, fork, and body placement;

**8.2 Contract.** Inputs are explicit (including the date — no hidden clock). Outputs are deterministic (§5.8), versioned (§5.9), carry the compliance framing (§9), and are rate-limited per caller. Access may be metered per request (the reference provider supports HTTP-native payment).

**8.3 Outcomes.** Where a person reports a before-and-after felt state around a session, that response may be recorded as a *recalibration response* (subjective, non-clinical). Aggregated responses inform future Standard versions; they are never used to make individual claims.
