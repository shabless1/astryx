Five rooms made of glass.

Every palette below is a complete set of four rooms — one for each state the engine can report. All of them are lit from behind, translucent, and saturated. None of them can go red.

What went wrong with the last one. I built that palette out of avoidance — the red rule told me where not to go and I retreated into warm neutrals at mid-saturation, which on deep space is the one place colour neither glows nor recedes. Dry Stone, Tallow, Warm Gray. A palette made of absences. Meanwhile the engine's own therapy colours — Blue-Green, Deep Indigo, Deep Violet, Gold Containment, Seafoam — had the soul in them the whole time. Never-amplify means don't feed the heat. It never meant be beige.
Every value here is law-safe — nothing sits in the red band (hue under 28° or over 335° at any real saturation), so no palette can flood a Mars or Pluto day with red. The difference from the last attempt is saturation: these run 0.50–0.87 where the stale set sat at 0.13–0.55.

Pick a palette and I'll wire it, carry the glass treatment across the real screens, and take the four room names into the house style. If one is close but the resting colour is off, say which and I'll re-tune that room only.
